1. Locate the Bill's Key Fields
Each target file is a single invoice document. Find the invoice details
block, which sits alongside the "Bill To" section and contains, in
order: Invoice #, PO #, Invoice Date, Tax Registration #, Tax File #,
and Commercial Registration #. Also locate the itemized table (Item
Name, Description, Price, Quantity, Total per line) and the totals
section (Subtotal, Tax, Total).

2. Check Tax Registration # and Commercial Registration #
Determine whether the Tax Registration # field has an actual value
filled in, and separately whether the Commercial Registration # field
has an actual value filled in. A field counts as missing if it is
blank, empty, or not present on the document at all -- whether or not
its label is shown.

3. Classify the Bill
If both the Tax Registration # and the Commercial Registration # have
values, classify the bill as REAL. If either one is missing, classify
the bill as FAKE.

4. Verify the Bill's Totals and Amounts
Independently of the REAL/FAKE classification, check the bill's
arithmetic:
   a. For each line item, confirm Price x Quantity equals that line's
      Total.
   b. Confirm the sum of every line item's Total equals the Subtotal.
   c. Confirm Subtotal + Tax equals the final Total.
If every one of these checks matches, the bill's amounts are considered
CONSISTENT. If any one of them does not match, the bill's amounts are
considered INCONSISTENT and the bill must be flagged for human review
as a financial discrepancy, regardless of what its REAL/FAKE
classification was.

5. Task for human auditor: review bills
Any bill classified as FAKE, or flagged as having INCONSISTENT amounts,
must be routed to a human auditor. The human auditor reviews the bill
directly -- including the vendor and client details, the item table,
which of the two registration fields was missing (if any), and which
arithmetic check failed (if any) -- and makes the final determination
of whether the bill is genuine or fake, and whether the amount
discrepancy is acceptable or must be corrected.

6. Audit Report Generation
Generate a final audit report stating the bill's classification (REAL
or FAKE), whether its amounts were CONSISTENT or INCONSISTENT, whether
either determination was made automatically or by a human auditor, and
the reasoning behind each.
