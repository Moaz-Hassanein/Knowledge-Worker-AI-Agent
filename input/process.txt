1. Invoice vs Purchase Order Review
For every vendor invoice received, compare it against the original
purchase order. Verify that the quantities delivered match the quantities
ordered, unit prices match the agreed contract price, the calculated
subtotal is correct, the sales tax applied matches the required rate for
the delivery region, and the final total matches the invoice grand total.
Any invoice with a discrepancy must be flagged and routed to the accounts
payable manager for manual approval before payment is released.

2. Inventory Reconciliation Review
At the end of each month, reconcile the warehouse inventory. Take the
previous month's ending inventory count, subtract all recorded sales and
dispatches for the current month, and add any recorded incoming
shipments. Compare this calculated figure to the physical inventory count
performed by warehouse staff. Any mismatch above the acceptable threshold
must be investigated and a discrepancy report generated identifying the
likely cause.

3. Employee Expense Report Review
Review each submitted employee expense report against the attached
receipts. Confirm that every claimed expense has a matching receipt, that
the expense category is appropriate for the employee's role, and that no
single expense exceeds the per-category spending limit. Reports with any
expense lacking a receipt or exceeding policy limits must be sent back to
the employee's manager for review before reimbursement is processed.
