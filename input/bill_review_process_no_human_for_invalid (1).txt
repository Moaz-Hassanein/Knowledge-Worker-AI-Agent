1. Locate the Bill's Key Fields
Each target file is a single invoice document. Find the invoice details
block, which sits alongside the "Bill To" section and contains, in
order: Invoice #, PO #, Invoice Date, Tax Registration #, Tax File #,
and Commercial Registration #. Also locate the itemized table (Item
Name, Description, Price, Quantity, Total per line) and the totals
section (Subtotal, Tax, Total).

2. Check Tax Registration # and Commercial Registration #
Determine whether the Tax Registration # field has an actual value
filled in, and separately whether the Commercial Registration # field
has an actual value filled in. A field counts as missing if it is
blank, empty, or not present on the document at all -- whether or not
its label is shown.

3. Classify the Bill
If both the Tax Registration # and the Commercial Registration # have
values, classify the bill as VALID. If either one is missing, classify
the bill as INVALID. This classification is recorded automatically --
no human review is triggered by it, whether the result is VALID or
INVALID.

4. Verify the Bill's Totals and Amounts
Independently of the VALID/INVALID classification, check the bill's
arithmetic:
   a. For each line item, confirm Price x Quantity equals that line's
      Total.
   b. Confirm the sum of every line item's Total equals the Subtotal.
   c. Confirm Subtotal + Tax equals the final Total.
If every one of these checks matches, the bill's amounts are considered
CONSISTENT and recorded automatically. If any one of them does not
match, the bill's amounts are considered INCONSISTENT and the bill must
be flagged for human review as a financial discrepancy, regardless of
what its VALID/INVALID classification was.

5. Task for human auditor: review bills
Only bills flagged as having INCONSISTENT amounts are routed to a human
auditor. A bill's VALID/INVALID classification alone never triggers
human review. When a bill is routed to a human auditor, they review the
bill directly -- including the vendor and client details, the item
table, and which arithmetic check failed -- and make the final
determination of whether the amount discrepancy is acceptable or must
be corrected.

6. Audit Report Generation
Generate a final audit report stating the bill's classification (VALID
or INVALID) -- always determined automatically -- and whether its
amounts were CONSISTENT (determined automatically) or INCONSISTENT
(determined by a human auditor), along with the reasoning behind each.
