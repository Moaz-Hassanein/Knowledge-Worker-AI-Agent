Policy 1: Approval and Escalation Thresholds
A quantity or inventory variance of up to 2% is acceptable without
escalation. Variances between 2% and 5% require supervisor sign-off.
Anything above 5%, or any unit price difference greater than $0.01, must
be escalated to the relevant department director.

Policy 2: Documentation and Spending Limits
Every reviewed transaction must reference its supporting document
(purchase order, receipt, or shipment record) or it is rejected outright.
Meals are capped at $60/day domestic and $90/day international, lodging
at $250/night, and any single expense over $500 requires a scanned
receipt regardless of category.
