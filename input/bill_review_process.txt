1. Locate the Bill's Key Fields
Each target file is a single invoice document. Find the invoice details
block, which sits alongside the "Bill To" section and contains, in
order: Invoice #, PO #, Invoice Date, Tax Registration #, Tax File #,
and Commercial Registration #.

2. Check Tax Registration # and Commercial Registration #
Determine whether the Tax Registration # field has an actual value
filled in, and separately whether the Commercial Registration # field
has an actual value filled in. A field counts as missing if it is
blank, empty, or not present on the document at all -- whether or not
its label is shown.

3. Classify the Bill
If both the Tax Registration # and the Commercial Registration # have
values, classify the bill as REAL. If either one is missing, classify
the bill as FAKE.

4. Task for human auditor: review bills
Any bill classified as FAKE must be routed to a human auditor. The
human auditor reviews the bill directly -- including the vendor and
client details, the item table, and which of the two fields was missing
-- and makes the final determination of whether the bill is genuine or
fake.

5. Audit Report Generation
Generate a final audit report stating the bill's classification (REAL
or FAKE), whether that classification was made automatically or by a
human auditor, and the reasoning behind it.
